FAQ

I bought Remove Ads, but ads came back after reinstalling.

Tap Settings › Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID.

My progress is gone.

Progress is saved only on your device. It is deleted with the app and does not transfer to other devices.

How do I see the controls again?

Settings › Tutorial lets you replay it at any time.

How do I change the arena?

On the title screen, tap Settings › Arena. After you beat the final opponent, the next tournament is held in the other arena.

How do I change my ad consent? (EEA / UK)

Settings › Privacy Options lets you change it at any time.
